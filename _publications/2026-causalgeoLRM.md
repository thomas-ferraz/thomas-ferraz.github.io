summary: >-
  Latent reasoning models can display human-observable patterns that look like reasoning but are not causally needed for the output, thus not evidence of mechanism. We argue that latent thoughts should be treated as hidden computation, not hidden explanation, and that LRM interpretability needs matched controls and causal interventions.
